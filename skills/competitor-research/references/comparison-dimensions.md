# Optional comparison dimensions

Choose dimensions that affect the customer's decision; this is a menu, not a mandatory eight-column table.

| Dimension | Evidence to look for |
|---|---|
| Positioning | Customer and outcome explicitly described by the product |
| Target behavior | Who uses it, in what situation, and how often |
| Workflow | Inputs, steps, output, and required effort |
| Monetization | Published prices, ads, commerce, or other visible revenue paths |
| Distribution and traction | Public download/review data, usage claims with provenance, update history |
| Technical dependency | AI/data source, required integrations, or user-provided material |
| Constraint | Cases or users the product does not serve well |
| Defensibility | Evidence of a hard-to-copy advantage, if any; early products may have none |

Mark a dimension **unknown** when the source does not support it. Do not infer revenue, active users, or technical architecture from marketing copy. Treat an adjacent-market product as an analogy unless the target customers demonstrably use it as a substitute.
