# org-diagnosis-pivot

## Goal

Find out the real state of a company or your own product with a 40-question diagnostic, then use those facts to decide whether to push on, pivot, or walk away.

## When to use

1. Something feels wrong at work and you want to pin down what exactly.
2. You are deciding whether to join a company, or checking fit early in a new role.
3. A product or business has stalled and you need to decide whether to persist or change course.
