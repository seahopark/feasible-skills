# Org/Business Diagnostic Question Bank (9 categories, 40 questions)

Pick categories selectively based on the situation — pre-join due diligence, in-tenure diagnosis, or stalled-business review.

## 1. Business Strategy — Is the strategy clear?
- What project/product initiative is currently in progress? Why was it prioritized first?
- What's the single most important metric for this service? Why?
- What's the thing the team needs to solve first? Why does it need to be first?
- What is the company most focused on right now — growing an existing line, or launching something new?
- Right now, is retention or acquisition the bigger priority? Why?
- What's the biggest current worry — resources, legal/regulatory, something else?

## 2. User Persona — Is it clear who this is for?
- Which user segment should this service be focused on right now? Why?
- What do these users have in common (problems, desires)?
- Is there a known user problem/desire that hasn't been addressed — and why not?

## 3. User Data — Is the user base understood through data?
- What metrics are known about users? What process produced them?
- Same as above but framed around resourcing: why hasn't a known problem/desire been addressed (resources? legal? other risk?)
- Who are the heaviest users of the service?
- Which user segment produces the most revenue (or the most of whatever the key metric is)?
- What's the single most important metric to know about that segment, and why?

## 4. Customer Communication — Is customer feedback actually heard?
- How is user feedback or complaints tracked?
- How many inquiries come in on an average day, and how are they handled? Is there a process for triage/response?

## 5. Product List — Is what's being built actually visible?
- Are there services that clearly aren't paying back their resource cost? Why are they still being kept alive?
- What products are currently in development or in production (including internal/back-office tools)?

## 6. Production Initiation — How does work get decided and started?
- What defines "done," and when is that bar decided?
- How does planning start once work kicks off?
- Have assumptions made during estimation turned out wrong? Why?
- What drives the decision to build a new feature or service? How does work actually start?
- What's the basis for scheduling/estimating work?

## 7. Production Process — Is there a real validation loop for design/spec/dev?
- How does design get validated?
- What happens when a spec doc still has undecided parts in it?
- Before development starts, how are deployment and operational concerns checked and scheduled?
- How does spec/planning get validated?
- Is there any end-to-end way to check on the overall state of planning/dev/design progress?

## 8. Production Management — Are priority and communication actually managed?
- How is development progress tracked? Any specific tool or method?
- What determines what gets done first?
- How are dev requests communicated? Has anything ever gotten dropped or lost in that handoff?

## 9. Development Process — Is dev/deploy process actually mature?
- How does testing work? What does the deployment process look like?
- Is the company's direction communicated transparently to the product team? Do people actually know the goal they're working toward?
- What does the process look like from dev kickoff through deployment?
- What tools are used for code-level communication with engineers? Does API documentation exist?

## Recommended category combos by situation
- **Evaluating a company to join**: 1 (Strategy) + 5 (Product List) + 9 (Development Process) — a fast read on direction, product portfolio, and dev maturity
- **Clarifying a vague in-tenure complaint**: pick freely across all categories based on what the complaint is actually about — 6, 7, 8 (the three Production categories) are especially good at turning "something feels off but I can't say what" into something concrete
- **Diagnosing a stalled product/business**: 1 (Strategy) + 2/3 (User Persona/Data) + 5 (Product List) — direction, user understanding, and portfolio health
