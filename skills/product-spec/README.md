# product-spec

## Goal

Reverse-engineer a product from screenshots, interviews, and hands-on use into two documents: Business Discovery (why it exists) and Product Spec (what it does).

## When to use

1. You joined a team and need to understand the product's structure.
2. Feature definitions for an existing product are missing or scattered.
3. You need to turn screenshots, Figma, or a live service into a spec.
